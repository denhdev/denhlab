I'm Dennis, an architect and designer who moved into software. These days I teach engineers how to run production workloads on Kubernetes and AWS, and in my own time I build the small, secondhand homelab this site is about.

## From buildings to clusters

I trained and worked as an architect and designer, and in 2019 I was teaching state-of-the-art fabrication (3D printing, CNC and laser cutting) at the Academy of Art University in San Francisco. In 2020 I moved into software as a full stack developer, right as the world locked down.

Since 2021 I've been a technical instructor:

- **2021**: training engineers in cloud-native deployments on Kubernetes at a major telecom company.
- **2022**: developing and teaching GitLab CI/CD pipelines with Terraform for AWS cloud-native deployments, with a Fortune 50 insurance company.
- **2024**: production-grade ROSA (Red Hat OpenShift Service on AWS) with Argo CD and External Secrets Operator.

Distributed computing turned out to scratch the same itch as architecture, which is how I ended up teaching it.

## Away from the keyboard

I live on Mercer Island, between Seattle and Bellevue, with my wife, our Boston Terrier and three lop rabbits.

I enjoy perfecting modlists for Stalker and Fallout, playing retro games on handhelds, rewatching the same thirty or so films, and slowly selling off the gear from old hobbies to pay for this one.
